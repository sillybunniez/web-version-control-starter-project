Union Pacific 844

The Living Legend

Built in December of 1944, UP 844 is a 4-8-4 Northern type FEF-3 Steam Locomotive. Today the locomotive still operates for its namesake railroad for special excursions.

Here are some fun facts about UP 844:

  • It has never been retired from service since it was built.
  • It used to burn coal but was converted earlier in it's service life to No.5 Fuel Oil.
  • It has 80 inch drivers.
